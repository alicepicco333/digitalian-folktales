      ii. how to read the edition

      the dialect text

      Typed in Courier, like the field record it comes from: the tale as it was told and written down, in Friulian or Sicilian.

      Quannau lu Maistru java caminannu cu li tridici Apostuli…

      the Italian translation

      Set beside it in a book serif, aligned passage by passage, so the eye can move across without losing its place.

      Mentre il Maestro camminava con i tredici Apostoli…

      the reading

      Each episode’s ATU tale type, the sequence of places, the people, the supernatural figures and the resolution, as recorded by the project team.

      ATU 774 · Countryside / road → Village 1 → Village 2

      iii. sources

      • RetellingItalo Calvino, Fiabe italiane: “Gesù e San Pietro in Friuli”; “Gesù e San Pietro in Sicilia”.
      • Friulian sourceDolfo Zorzùt, Sot la nape… (I racconti del popolo friulano), 3 voll., Udine 1924, 1925, 1927.
      • Sicilian sourceFiabe, novelle e racconti popolari siciliani, raccolti e illustrati da Giuseppe Pitrè, 4 voll., Palermo 1875. Biblioteca delle tradizioni popolari siciliane, voll. IV–VII.
      • Friulian texts usedCatalogo di Racconti Popolari Friulani, Società Filologica Friulana — raccontipopolari.filologicafriulana.it
      • Tale typesAarne–Thompson–Uther (ATU) index of tale types.
